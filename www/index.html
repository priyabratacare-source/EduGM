About EduGM UniTools

Your Everyday Digital Toolkit

EduGM UniTools is a collection of useful digital utilities designed to make everyday document, image and student tasks easier.

Our features

  • PDF and document utilities
  • Image conversion and resizing
  • Student calculators and helpers
  • QR code generation
  • Privacy-conscious local file processing

Privacy

Files selected for supported tools are processed on your device. Contact messages are submitted to our Firebase database. Do not send passwords, payment details or sensitive personal information through Contact Us.
