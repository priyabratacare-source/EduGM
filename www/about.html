Making student utilities easier.

EduGM UniTools is an educational utility platform designed to make common academic calculations and student document tasks easier and more accessible.

The platform brings together useful calculators and productivity utilities in one simple interface.

🎯

Our Mission

To make useful educational tools accessible to students through a simple and convenient platform.

💡

Our Approach

We focus on simplicity, usability and tools that solve common student problems.
